# About Clyde

Clyde turns your Linear tickets and your chats into pull requests that pass
your project's gates. It runs on your Mac mini, and you reach it from the Clyde
desktop app and the Clyde iOS app over your tailnet.

## What you can do

- Chat with an agent about one of your projects.
- Hand a Linear ticket to Clyde and get a pull request back.
- Talk with a running ticket from Linear or from the app.
- See the result and the log of each gate.
- Change a project's workflow.

## What it is not

- A hosted service. Clyde runs on your own machines.
- A team tool. Clyde has one user: you.
- A tracker. Clyde works from Linear only.
- A browser app. Clyde has a desktop app and an iOS app.

## How it works

The Clyde server runs on the Mac mini. The apps on your laptops and your
iPhone connect to it over the tailnet. Agents for ticket runs work in sandboxes
on the Spark. Linear sends Clyde its tickets, and GitHub gets its pull
requests.
